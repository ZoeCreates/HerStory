# HerStory Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

HerStory does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **No accounts.** You never sign up or log in.
- **No tracking or analytics.** The app contains no third-party SDKs, ads, or analytics.
- **Everything stays on your device.** Your saved quotes, chosen topics, theme, and reminder time are stored only on your iPhone (and its widget). They are never sent to us or anyone else.
- **Notifications.** Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No server is involved.
- **Photos.** If you tap "Save to Photos", HerStory asks for permission to add the wallpaper image to your photo library. It never reads your existing photos.

If you delete the app, all of its data is deleted with it.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/ZoeCreates/HerStory/issues.
